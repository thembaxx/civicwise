# Civic Ledger

Civic Ledger is an independent South African civic utility. It helps residents understand municipal finances, find their IEC ward, and maintain a timestamped history of user-submitted service issues.

## Product truth
- Municipal finance data comes from the National Treasury Municipal Money API and is presented with source context.
- User-submitted issue reports are portable records owned by the resident.
- A report is not evidence that a municipality received, acknowledged, or resolved an issue unless that status is explicitly confirmed.
- Ward boundaries are based on IEC boundary data. Ward councillor and ward budget details should be labelled with their source and freshness.

## Core routes
- `/` overview and followed municipality
- `/m` municipality and ward lookup
- `/m/[code]` municipality dashboard
- `/report` create a timestamped issue report
- `/reports` issue history
- `/plans` paid plan information

## Audience
Residents, civic groups, journalists, and researchers in South Africa.
